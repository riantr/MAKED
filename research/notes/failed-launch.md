# What We Learned From The Failed Launch

We propose a new method for reaching the market, but the method is not the
interesting part. The interesting part is what the launch taught us.

The launch failed at scale. The funnel was fine, the copy was fine, and the
moment real traffic arrived the thing we had called ready could not answer.
Every number we had was from a rehearsal.

What we would do differently:

- Rehearse with the shape of the real thing, not a friendly version of it.
- Count the thing that broke, not the thing that worked.
- Write down the assumption that was never tested while it is still cheap to
  change.
